Qoua Her

Pharmacist, Informatician, and Pharmacoepidemiologist

I am an NIDDK-funded postdoctoral research fellow and PhD student in Epidemiology at the Gillings School of Global Public Health, UNC, under the mentorship of Drs. Til Stürmer, Michele Jonsson-Funk, John Buse, and Jessica Edwards. My research focuses on applied epidemiologic methods to support valid pharmacovigilance and pharmacoepidemiology studies.

I am also a Pharmacist Informatician at the Center for Medication Safety, Department of Veterans Affairs (VA MedSAFE).

Prior to UNC, I worked at Harvard Pilgrim Health Care Institute and Brigham and Women’s Hospital, with experience as a pharmacist in community and acute care settings.

Research in Progress

My work focuses on generating rigorous real-world evidence to inform medication safety, effectiveness, and regulatory decision-making.

  • Drug Safety & Effectiveness

    Evaluating post-market safety signals and comparative effectiveness of medications using large healthcare databases and advanced epidemiologic methods.

  • Biosimilars & Real-World Evidence

    Studying utilization, switching, and safety of biosimilars to support regulatory science and evidence-based adoption.

  • Drug Deprescribing

    Developing and validating methods to estimate the causal effects of treatment discontinuation and dose reduction in observational data.

Software & Tools

I develop and maintain software and tools to support pharmacoepidemiology, real-world evidence, and drug safety research.

  • rtreeScan R package

    An R package for TreeScan analysis of safety signals in healthcare data. CRAN

  • Deprescribing Simulation Scripts

    Custom R scripts for simulating and evaluating drug deprescribing strategies using observational data.

  • Data Management Tools

    Scripts and pipelines to clean, merge, and prepare large administrative and claims datasets for analysis.
